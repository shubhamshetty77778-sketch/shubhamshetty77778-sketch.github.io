              Visitors are counted once a day per device; a visit is one browsing session. Time on site counts only while the site is on screen. Videos count when played in the site's own player. "Returning" means the same phone or browser has been here before. Bots and your own devices are left out (this device is marked as yours now that you've opened this page). Country, state and (for India) city come from the visitor's internet connection, so they can be off near borders or on mobile data, where the city is often the network's nearest hub; the address itself is never stored.
